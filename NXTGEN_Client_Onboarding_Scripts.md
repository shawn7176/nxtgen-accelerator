# NXTGEN Client Onboarding & Communication Scripts
### Business Credit & Federal Program — 6-Month Engagement

**How to use this document:** Everything inside the shaded "SEND AS EMAIL" blocks is written to be copy-pasted directly into Gmail as plain text — no markdown, section dividers only, matching how we send everything else client-facing. The call script is for Kathleen to talk from live, not to send. Anything in [BRACKETS] is a variable to fill in per client before sending.

Every introductory email includes the SAM.gov federal registration track as a parallel opportunity — that's a standing requirement, not optional, for every new client.

- The exact address the IRS has on file, and the address on your state filing (if they're different, that's common — we'll fix it on the call)

ONE IMPORTANT NOTE ON SENSITIVE INFORMATION
----------------------------------------
If anything in this process requires a Social Security Number (for example, some vendor applications ask for one to verify identity), we only ever collect that by phone, live, on a call. That last one runs in parallel with the credit build, not after it — we like to get your federal registration moving early since SAM.gov activation takes a couple of weeks on its own. This includes your UEI and SAM.gov registration (both always free from the government — we never mark those up), plus grant intelligence scanning and proposal writing, which are part of your program at no extra cost.

----------------------------------------
WHAT WE NEED FROM YOU
----------------------------------------
Throughout the program, the fastest way to keep things moving is a quick reply when we ask for a signature, a document, or an approval on a vendor purchase. We'll never surprise you with a charge — every vendor order is discussed with you first.

Talk soon,

## 2. Kickoff Call Script (Phone — Not Sent)
**When:** First live call, ideally within 3 business days of purchase. Sensitive info (SSN, if needed) is collected here, verbally, never in writing.

**Opening (2 min)**
"Hi [NAME], thanks for making time — excited to get started. This call has two goals: I want to get your business details locked down exactly right, since that's the foundation everything else builds on, and I want to walk you through what the next six months actually look like so there are no surprises. Sound good?"

**Confirm identity basics (5–10 min)**
- "What's the exact legal name on your formation documents? I want it word-for-word, including LLC/Inc."
- "What's your EIN?"
- "What address does the IRS have on file for you? And what address is on your state filing?"
  - If they differ: "This is really common, and it's actually the single biggest thing that trips up a credit build if we don't fix it now. I'm going to recommend we standardize on [RECOMMENDED ADDRESS] — here's why: [reason]. Does that work, or is there a reason you need the other one?"
- "Do you have a business bank account already, in the exact legal name?"
- "Do you already have a D-U-N-S number, or has anyone applied for one on your behalf?"

**Sensitive information, if needed (only if a specific vendor or registration requires it)**
"A couple of the accounts we'll open ask for an SSN to verify identity — this is the only time I'll ever ask you for this, and I'm asking live on the phone specifically so it's never sitting in an email or a form. Can you give me that now?"
[Collect verbally. Never type into chat, email, or an unencrypted note.]

**Explain the roadmap (5 min)**
"Here's the order we work in, and roughly when you'll hear from me at each step:" [walk through Phases 1–6 from the welcome email, in plain language]

**Set expectations (3 min)**
- "You'll get a short email every time a new phase kicks off, so you always know where we are."
- "If I ever need something from you — a signature, an approval on a vendor purchase — I'll flag it clearly and it'll usually take you two minutes."
- "We monitor everything, so you'll never have to wonder whether an account actually reported — I'll tell you when it does."

**Close**
"That's everything I need for today. Next thing you'll hear from me is the Phase 1 kickoff email, usually within [X] business days. Any questions before we wrap up?"
