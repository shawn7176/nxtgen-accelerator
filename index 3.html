Questions, answered

Frequently asked.

Is this credit repair?

No. This is business credit building, done for you — establishing and managing a new, legitimate credit file for your company with real reporting vendors. We do not dispute items on personal credit reports, and we never use CPNs or misrepresent business age or revenue.

What exactly does NXTGEN do vs. what do I do?

We execute the build: opening bureau files, selecting and managing vendor accounts, running the early-payment cadence, filing your federal registration, and handling grant matching and writing. You provide the business documents and decisions we need along the way (e.g., signing applications, approving payments) — we handle the rest.

How long until my business has a usable score?

There is no legitimate overnight score. With clean sequencing, first tradelines typically post within 1–3 months of opening vendor accounts, and a usable PAYDEX generally follows once enough payment experiences report — all within your 6-month program.

Are grants really included, not extra?

Yes. Grant Intelligence Scanning and Grant Writing (up to 2 proposals/month) are part of every program at no additional charge — most competitors charge $250–$1,200/month for this alone.

Can I pay monthly?

Yes — 6 monthly payments of $2,083 (totaling $12,500), or pay in full for $10,995 and save more.

Is SAM.gov registration really free?

Yes — always. The UEI, SAM.gov registration, CAGE code, and renewals cost nothing from the government. We handle the entire filing as part of your program at no markup. Anyone billing separately for "required" federal registration is not legitimate.

Will this get my business $50K guaranteed?

No, and be suspicious of anyone who promises that. This program builds the file that funding decisions are made on. Approvals depend on your revenue, time in business, and the strength of the profile — no one can guarantee a lending outcome.

What if a vendor stops reporting?

We monitor every tradeline and handle vendor substitutions on your behalf — that's part of what "done for you" means here.

What's the refund and cancellation policy?

Full details are in our Refund Policy. In short: you can cancel before setup work begins for a full refund; once we've begun filing registrations and opening accounts on your behalf, one-time setup fees reflect work already performed, and any remaining monthly installments are simply not charged if you cancel.

Our hard lines — what we will never do
  • No CPNs or "credit privacy numbers" in place of an EIN or SSN where one is legally required.
  • No misrepresenting time-in-business, revenue, or shelf-company age on any application — that's fraud, not strategy.
  • No paid "personal credit repair" — that's a separately regulated activity (CROA + state law), and it's outside this program's scope.
  • No paying third parties for government registrations that are free.
