Questions, answered

Frequently asked.

Is this credit repair?

No. This is business credit building and education — establishing a new, legitimate credit file for your company with real reporting vendors. We do not dispute items on personal credit reports, and we never use CPNs or misrepresent business age or revenue.

How long until my business has a usable score?

There is no legitimate overnight score. With clean sequencing, first tradelines typically post within 1–3 months of opening vendor accounts, and a usable PAYDEX generally follows once enough payment experiences report. Speed comes from removing dead time between steps — not from shortcuts.

Do I need an LLC first?

You need a registered entity, an EIN, and a business bank account — Module 1 walks through getting the identity layer exactly right before anything else. If you're not formed yet, the module covers what to lock down at formation so you don't create problems later.

Will this get my business $50K guaranteed?

No, and be suspicious of anyone who promises that. This program builds the file that funding decisions are made on. Approvals depend on your revenue, time in business, and the strength of the profile you build — no one can guarantee a lending outcome.

Is SAM.gov registration really free?

Yes — always. The UEI, SAM.gov registration, CAGE code, and renewals cost nothing from the government. Any email, call, or company demanding payment for a "required" registration is not legitimate. Module 6 walks the whole process step by step.

What if a vendor stops reporting?

Reporting relationships change — that's why the program builds verification into every step. You'll monitor each tradeline, confirm it posts, and know exactly when to substitute a vendor. The vendor matrix bonus is maintained as things change.

Our hard lines — what we will never teach
  • No CPNs or "credit privacy numbers" in place of an EIN or SSN where one is legally required.
  • No misrepresenting time-in-business, revenue, or shelf-company age on any application — that's fraud, not strategy.
